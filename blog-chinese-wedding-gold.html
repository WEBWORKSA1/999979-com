Chinese wedding gold guide: 三金, 五金 & dragon-phoenix bangles

By the 999979.com editorial team · Updated 2026-09-30

Gold is at the heart of many Chinese weddings. Here's what is traditional, typical weights, and how to budget at 2026 prices without overpaying.

The sets

  • 三金 three golds: necklace, ring, earrings.
  • 五金 five golds: the three golds plus a bracelet or bangle and a pendant.
  • 龙凤镯 dragon-phoenix bangles: a matched pair for harmony, common at Cantonese weddings.

How much gold?

Families choose very different weights. A modest 三金 set might be 15–30 g, while generous 五金 sets with heavy bangles can pass 100 g. Pick a budget first, then a weight. A 40 g 足金 set at today's price →

Save money without losing meaning

  1. Split metal and labour. Ask for the price per gram and the making charge (工费) separately.
  2. Choose 足金 for daily-wear pieces. 千足金 matters most for bars.
  3. Pick a lucky weight. 9 g, 18 g or 99 g carries the symbolism at a lower cost than "bigger".
