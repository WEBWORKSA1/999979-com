Gold value calculator: what is my gold worth?

Enter the weight and purity to get the melt value at today's price. You'll also see what pawn shops, jewellers and refiners typically offer, and how much you could leave on the table.

How the calculation works

  1. Convert the weight to grams (1 troy oz = 31.1035 g).
  2. Multiply by purity to get the grams of fine gold.
  3. Multiply by the spot price per gram in your currency.

Example: 10 g of 22K (916) = 9.16 g fine gold × spot per gram.

5 ways to get more when you sell

  1. Know your number. Walk in with the melt value from this page.
  2. Separate by karat. Mixed lots get priced at the lowest purity.
  3. Weigh at home. A 0.01 g scale costs less than one bad deal.
  4. Keep receipts and certificates. Branded bars and coins get tighter spreads.
