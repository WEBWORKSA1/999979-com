Frequently asked questions

What is 9999 gold?
9999 (or 999.9) gold is 99.99% pure. It's the standard for investment bars, such as the Shanghai Gold Exchange's Au99.99 contract. In Chinese jewellery, the national grades are 足金 (≥99.0%) and 千足金 (≥99.9%).
How is the gold price on this site calculated?
We pull the international spot price (USD per troy ounce) and live exchange rates, then convert by weight and purity. Retail prices include premiums and making charges, so shop prices are higher and buy-back offers are lower than spot.
