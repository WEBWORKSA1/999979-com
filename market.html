What the data says

  • Investment has overtaken adornment. Chinese bar and coin demand jumped 28% to 432 t in 2025. Jewellery tonnage fell 25%, yet spending on it still rose 8%, because prices climbed so far.
  • ETFs went mainstream. Chinese gold ETF assets more than tripled, and Q4 2025 had the strongest inflows on record (¥51 bn).
  • The central bank is a steady buyer. The People's Bank of China extended its buying streak to roughly 22 consecutive months by August 2026.
  • Young buyers are in. 62% of Chinese aged 18–24 own gold jewellery, up from 37% in 2019. 79% of gold jewellery buyers buy for themselves (World Gold Council).
  • Volatility is real. Gold has fallen 9% in a single session in 2026. Never invest money you may need soon.

Sources: China Daily / WGC, World Gold Council, IndexBox, Caixin, Fortune.
