How to read these gold prices

Spot price is the wholesale price of one troy ounce of pure gold, set by trading in London, New York and Shanghai. Everything else comes from it: price per gram = spot ÷ 31.1035, multiplied by purity (e.g. 0.916 for 22K) and the exchange rate.

Why the jeweller's price is higher

  • Premium: dealer margin over spot, usually 2–8% for bars and coins.
  • Making charge (工费): labour for jewellery, charged per gram or as a percentage.
  • Taxes: VAT or GST applies in many countries.

Why buy-back offers are lower

Buyers deduct refining, assay and profit. The gap is widest at pawn shops and mail-in "cash for gold" services. It's narrowest at refiners and bullion dealers. Run the calculator to see your range.
