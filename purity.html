Chinese stamps explained

China's national standard GB 11887 sets the naming for precious-metal jewellery. 足金 means at least 990‰ gold. 千足金 means at least 999‰. You will also see 万足金 ("ten-thousand-fine", 9999). That is a trade and marketing term tied to investment-grade 99.99% gold, not a separate national jewellery grade. Treat it with care and ask for an assay certificate.

Stamps that mean "not solid gold"

  • GP / GEP / HGE / 镀金: gold-plated or electroplated.
  • GF / RGP / 包金: gold-filled or rolled gold, a thicker layer but still mostly base metal.
  • 925 / S925 / 银: silver. Pt / 铂: platinum.

Stamps can be faked. For high-value items, use an XRF test at a reputable buyer, or an independent assay.
